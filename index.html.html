📅 What is the Past Simple?

We use the Past Simple to talk about completed actions in the past — actions that are finished and happened at a specific time.

Subject + Verb (past form) + rest of sentence
I played football yesterday.
She ate a pizza last night.
They went to the cinema.
He studied for the test.

✅ Regular Verbs: add -ED

For most verbs, just add -ed to the base form. Some spelling rules apply!

play → played  |  watch → watched  |  study → studied
walk → walked
jump → jumped
stop → stopped (double p!)
love → loved (just +d)
try → tried (y → ied)
dance → danced

⚡ Irregular Verbs — No Rules!

These verbs are rebels — they don't follow the -ed rule. You have to learn them by heart! 💪

❓ Negatives & Questions with DID

For negatives and questions in the Past Simple, we use the auxiliary verb DID / DIDN'T. The main verb returns to its base form!

❌ She didn't go to school. (NOT didn't went)
❓ Did he eat? — Yes, he did. / No, he didn't.
I didn't watch TV.
Did you like it?
They didn't play.
Did she run?
